# Niche communities (~30) — tailored angle


## Embed distribution (draft)

BasicLaw’s **iframe embeds** (`/embed/ask`, `/embed/audit`, `/embed/loader.js`) are aimed at sites that already publish rights education but want **interactive** jurisdiction context without building their own legal AI.

**Primary embed targets**

- **Tenant unions & legal aid coalitions** (US, UK, EU): housing rights pages with an inline “Ask BasicLaw” next to notice-period explainers.
- **Immigration & refugee NGOs**: intake microsites where a short Q&A widget answers “what does the constitution say about X?” in plain language with citations.
- **University law clinics & Street Law–style programs**: clinic blogs embedding the **audit** widget for red-flag demos on sample clauses.
- **International human-rights hubs**: country-specific resource pages linking compare + embed for one-click follow-up questions.
- **Diaspora community forums** (NG, GH, KE, ZA, IN): constitution overview articles with embed for “ask in your words.”
- **Public-library digital literacy partners**: embed on “know your rights” portals with strict attribution and noindex-safe iframe routes.

**Pitch line:** “Drop one script tag — readers stay on your page, answers stay citation-grounded to BasicLaw’s library.”
